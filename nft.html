NFT Basics

What makes an NFT unique?

NFT stands for Non-Fungible Token. Unlike Bitcoin (where every coin is identical), each NFT is one-of-a-kind — its ownership and provenance permanently recorded on a blockchain.

🖼️

Digital Art & Collectibles

Artists tokenise their work as NFTs, giving buyers verifiable ownership. Platforms like OpenSea have traded billions in digital art. Think of it as the internet's art market.

🎮

Gaming & Virtual Items

In-game characters, weapons, and land parcels as NFTs. Players truly own their assets — tradeable across marketplaces. Axie Infinity, Gods Unchained, Illuvium.

🎵

Music & Royalties

Musicians release tracks as NFTs, giving holders rights to royalty streams. Platforms like Sound.xyz let fans co-own music and earn from its success.

🏠

Real-World Assets (RWA)

Tokenise real estate, luxury goods, or event tickets as NFTs. Fractional ownership of physical assets becomes possible — even a RM 2M house can be split into 2,000 NFTs.

🪪

Identity & Credentials

Soulbound tokens (SBTs) — non-transferable NFTs — for diplomas, KYC passes, and community memberships. Your on-chain identity, permanently yours.

₿

Bitcoin Ordinals

NFTs inscribed directly onto individual satoshis on Bitcoin. No smart contracts needed — data lives on the most secure blockchain forever. Growing rapidly in Malaysia.

Getting Started

How to buy your first NFT

01

Set Up a Wallet

MetaMask for Ethereum NFTs. Phantom or Solflare for Solana. UniSat for Bitcoin Ordinals. Never share your seed phrase.

02

Get the Right Crypto

Buy ETH from Luno or MX Global (available in Malaysia). Transfer to your wallet. Keep extra for gas fees.

03

Research the Collection

Check Twitter, Discord, roadmap, team doxxing, smart contract audit. Floor price history on DappRadar or NFTGo.

04

Connect & Buy

Go to the marketplace. Connect wallet. Click "Buy Now" or place a bid. Confirm the transaction and gas fee.

05

Store Safely

For high-value NFTs, transfer to a hardware wallet (Ledger). Never click unknown links or approve unknown contracts.

06

Track & Sell

Use NFTGo or Compass to track floor prices. List on OpenSea or Blur when ready to sell. Set royalty-aware listings.
