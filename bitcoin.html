Bitcoin Ordinals

NFTs on the most secure blockchain

Ordinals theory assigns a serial number to every satoshi and allows inscribing data directly onto them using Bitcoin's SegWit witness data — creating immutable, Bitcoin-native digital artifacts.

🔢
Ordinal Theory
Casey Rodarmor, 2023

Every satoshi (0.00000001 BTC) is numbered in the order it was mined. This numbering allows tracking individual sats as they move through transactions. Rare sats (first mined in an epoch, block, etc.) have collector value.

Inscriptions embed arbitrary content — images, HTML, JavaScript, video — into a sat's witness data. The inscription is stored on-chain forever, on Bitcoin's base layer, with no external dependencies.

🪙
BRC-20 & Runes
Token Standards on Bitcoin

BRC-20 uses JSON inscriptions to define fungible tokens on Bitcoin. Experimental but has produced billions in trading volume. Tokens like ORDI and SATS have multi-billion market caps.

Runes (by Casey Rodarmor) is a cleaner fungible token standard that stores balances in the UTXO set. Launched at the 2024 halving block — designed to replace BRC-20 with a more efficient model.

Advanced Bitcoin Tech

RGB Protocol & Bitcoin Stamps

🌈
RGB Protocol
Client-Side Smart Contracts on Bitcoin + Lightning

RGB is a smart contract system that uses Bitcoin and Lightning as a settlement and transport layer. Unlike Ethereum, validation happens client-side — only the parties involved know the contract state. This makes RGB extremely private and scalable.

RGB supports fungible tokens (RGB20), non-fungible assets (RGB21), and complex programmable contracts through its AluVM virtual machine. Assets can be sent over Lightning Network channels with instant finality.

Use cases: issuing stablecoins on Bitcoin, private NFTs, decentralised identity, tokenised securities — all without smart contract risk visible on-chain.

Bitcoin Stamps (SRC-20)
Immutable on-chain data storage

Bitcoin Stamps use the UTXO set (transaction outputs) to store data, rather than the witness data used by Ordinals. The key difference: UTXO data cannot be pruned by full nodes, making Stamps truly permanent — even if a node runs in pruned mode.

The SRC-20 token standard (analogous to BRC-20 for Ordinals) allows creating fungible tokens with Stamps. KEVIN, STAMP, and PEPE are among the most traded SRC-20 tokens.

Stamp images are encoded in Base64 and embedded across transaction outputs. They're typically smaller files due to UTXO size constraints — making them distinct pixel-art style collectibles.

Bitcoin History

From Satoshi to global ecosystem

2008
Bitcoin Whitepaper
Satoshi Nakamoto publishes "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" on Halloween. The birth of the entire crypto industry.
2009
Genesis Block Mined
Block 0 mined on 3 January 2009 with the message: "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks."
2017
Lightning Network & SegWit
Segregated Witness (SegWit) activates, enabling Lightning Network. Scalability solutions begin shipping.
2021
Taproot Upgrade
Bitcoin's most significant upgrade in years. Enables Schnorr signatures, MAST, and sets the foundation for Ordinals and RGB Protocol.
2023
Ordinals & BRC-20 Explosion
Casey Rodarmor launches Ordinal theory. Inscriptions hit 10M within months. BRC-20 tokens create billions in trading volume. Bitcoin's NFT moment.
2024
4th Halving & Runes Launch
Block reward drops to 3.125 BTC. Runes protocol launches at the halving block. Bitcoin ETF approvals by SEC bring institutional capital.
2025+
Bitcoin L2 Wars
Stacks sBTC, BOB, Merlin, Citrea, Taproot Assets — the race to bring DeFi to Bitcoin security is on. Malaysia's builder community is joining.
