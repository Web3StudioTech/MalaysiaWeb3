What is an Airdrop?

Free tokens — but not free money

An airdrop is when a blockchain project distributes tokens directly to wallet addresses — usually to reward early users, bootstrap a community, or decentralise ownership. Understanding them is the first step to profiting from them.

🎁

Retroactive Airdrops

Rewards for past usage. If you used a protocol before it had a token, you may qualify. Examples: Uniswap, dYdX, Arbitrum.

🏗️

Task-Based Airdrops

Complete specific actions — bridge assets, provide liquidity, trade volume, or hold an NFT — to accumulate "points" that convert to tokens at TGE.

🗳️

Governance Airdrops

Distributed to give community members voting power in DAOs. Holding the token lets you vote on protocol upgrades, treasury usage, and more.

🔒

Staking & LP Rewards

Stake tokens or provide liquidity to earn ongoing emissions. These are the most predictable form — you earn as long as you participate.

⚠️

Airdrop Risks

Scam airdrops steal wallet approvals. Never connect your main wallet to unknown sites. Always verify contract addresses via official channels.

High Risk Area
🇲🇾

Tax in Malaysia

Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri (LHDN) may treat airdrop income as taxable gains. Consult a tax professional before claiming large airdrops.

How to Farm Airdrops

5 steps to systematic airdrop farming

Find & Research

Identify projects pre-token with active communities. Check Twitter, Telegram, DeFiLlama, and this page.

Set Up Wallets

Use a dedicated farming wallet. Never use your main holdings wallet. Hardware wallet for storage.

Earn Points

Trade, bridge, provide liquidity, or hold NFTs as specified by the project's point system.

Track & Calculate

Use our calculator below to estimate potential value based on FDV assumptions and your point rank.

Claim & Manage

At TGE, claim via official site only. Decide: hold, sell, or provide liquidity. Track cost basis for tax.

💡

Pro tip: Focus on quality over quantity. Farming 3–5 high-conviction projects deeply outperforms spreading thin across 20+ protocols. Consistent volume, diverse assets bridged, and early participation matter most for retroactive qualifying.
